3. THE SOFTWARE DOES NOT COLLECT DATA AUTOMATICALLY

The office applications part of the Software runs locally on the
User's device.

The Operator does not automatically collect, transfer, store or
analyze:

- data about the User's computer or other device;
- the IP address;
- operating system information;
- unique device identifiers;
- information about installed programs;
- the history of work in the Software;
- data on the use of Software features;
- the contents of documents created or opened by the User;
- technical logs, error and crash reports;
- diagnostic information;
- analytics and telemetry data.

The Software contains no tracking, advertising, advertising
identifiers, hidden statistics collection or other mechanisms designed
to gather information about the User or their device.

Documents and work results saved by the User on their device are
stored on the User's device or in the storage chosen by the User. The
Operator does not access them unless the User sends them to the
support service on their own initiative.


4. WHAT DATA IS PROCESSED AND WHY

4.1. The User's e-mail address is used and required to confirm the
license: for registration in the license dashboard
(officevega.com/dashboard), issuing and verifying the license key,
sending service messages necessary for the license to work
(confirmation, access recovery, notifications of material changes),
and for handling the User's requests.

4.2. When an invoice for a Corporate License is requested, the
following may additionally be processed: name, phone number or
telegram, payment details and data about the User's organization.

4.3. E-mail addresses and the stated information are not used for
advertising mailings without the User's separate consent.

4.4. Legal grounds for processing: the User's consent; the necessity
to perform the agreement with the User (granting the license); the
necessity to perform obligations established by the legislation of the
Russian Federation; the Operator's legitimate interests provided that
such processing does not violate the User's rights and freedoms.


5. STORAGE AND TRANSFER OF DATA

5.1. Data is stored on a server located on the territory of the
Russian Federation.

5.2. Data is not transferred to third parties, is not sold and is not
provided for advertising purposes — except in cases expressly provided
for by the legislation of the Russian Federation or required to comply
with the demands of authorized state bodies in the manner prescribed
by law.

5.3. The Operator takes the necessary legal, organizational and
technical measures to protect personal data: access restriction,
protected server infrastructure on the territory of the Russian
Federation, protected communication channels, access control,
granting access only to authorized persons.


6. THE VEGA AI PROVIDER SERVICE — A SEPARATE AGREEMENT

6.1. The VEGA AI provider service (api.vega.chat) is a separate
service with its own offer. The personal data processing policy
applying to its use is determined by that service's agreement.

6.2. The User accepts the VEGA service's offer and personal data
processing policy independently at the registration stage in the
api.vega.chat service. Without registration, the VEGA service
features are unavailable.

6.3. The Software itself works without registration; registration is
required only for the VEGA service and for the license dashboard.

6.4. Personal data anonymization. When the built-in VEGA AI provider
is used, the document context and the prompt (the User's request
text) are automatically anonymized before being sent to the model:
personal data (full names, phone numbers and the like) is masked in
the transferred fragments. Both document and file fragments and the
User's prompts are subject to anonymization.

Anonymization removes the risks of transferring personal data;
however, data that is not personal enters the model unchanged. The
User independently determines the composition of the transferred data
and must not send restricted-access information (state secrets, trade
secrets in violation of their protection regime, and the like) to the
AI features — see section 8 of the public offer.

When third-party AI providers are connected (corporate, local,
foreign), the VEGA service's anonymization is not applied, and data is
processed under the rules of the corresponding provider.

7.1. Data is processed for the duration of the license or until the
User withdraws consent, unless further processing is required under
the legislation of the Russian Federation. Once the purposes of
processing have been achieved, the data is subject to destruction or
depersonalization within the time limits established by law.

7.2. The User has the right to: receive information about the
processing of their data; request the rectification, blocking or
destruction of data; withdraw consent; appeal the Operator's actions
to the authorized body or to a court; send a request to the Operator
at mail@vega.chat.

7.3. Withdrawal of consent may make further use of the license
impossible if data processing is required to confirm it. Withdrawal
of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out
before the withdrawal.
